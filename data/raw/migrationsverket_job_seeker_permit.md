# Residence permit to seek employment or explore starting a business after studies

If you have completed higher education studies in Sweden lasting at least two semesters (full-time, corresponding to 60 ECTS credits), you can apply for a residence permit to seek employment or investigate business opportunities in Sweden.

## Key Eligibility Conditions
1. **Completed Degree:** You must have completed your comprehensive degree program (e.g., Master's degree or Bachelor's degree) from a Swedish university.
2. **Timely Application:** You must submit your application *before* your existing student residence permit expires. You cannot apply from abroad or after your study permit has expired.
3. **Passport Validity:** You must possess a valid passport covering the entire 12-month period requested.
4. **Comprehensive Health Insurance:** You must have full comprehensive health insurance coverage valid in Sweden.

## Maintenance Requirement
You must prove that you have enough funds to support yourself for the full 12-month period without needing welfare assistance.
- The financial maintenance requirement is currently **SEK 10,314 per month**, totaling **SEK 123,768** for the full 12 months.
- The funds must be available in your own liquid bank account.

## Transitioning to a Work Permit
Once you receive a valid job offer in Sweden during this 12-month job seeker period:
- You can apply for a regular Swedish work permit (*arbetstillstånd*) from within Sweden without leaving the country.
- You are allowed to begin working as soon as the work permit application has been submitted, provided your job seeker permit is still valid.
