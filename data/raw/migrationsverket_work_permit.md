# Work permit requirements in Sweden (Arbetstillstånd)

To work in Sweden as a non-EU/EEA citizen, you must obtain a work permit. An employer in Sweden must initiate the work permit application by creating an offer of employment (*anställningserbjudande*).

## General Requirements for a Work Permit
1. **Valid Passport:** You must hold a valid passport.
2. **Employment Offer:** You must have a written job offer with terms of employment that are on par with Swedish collective agreements (*kollektivavtal*) or what is customary within the occupation or industry.
3. **Salary Maintenance Threshold (Lönekrav):** The monthly gross salary must be at least **80 percent of the Swedish median salary** published by Statistics Sweden (SCB). As of recent updates, this threshold is **SEK 28,480 per month** (subject to periodic annual updates).
4. **Insurance Package:** The employer must provide occupational pension insurance, health insurance, life insurance, and occupational injury insurance from the start date of employment.
5. **Union Notification:** The relevant Swedish trade union (*fackförbund*) must have been given the opportunity to give an opinion on the employment terms.
6. **EU/EEA Advertising Rule:** For new hires directly from outside the EU, the position must have been advertised on the Swedish Public Employment Service (Arbetsförmedlingen / EURES) portal for at least 10 calendar days before the offer was made.

## Applying for Permanent Residency after Work Permits
A person who has held a work permit in Sweden for at least **four years (48 months)** within the previous seven years may be eligible to apply for a permanent residence permit (*permanent uppehållstillstånd - PUT*) when applying for an extension, provided they meet financial self-sufficiency requirements and pass character/conduct evaluations.
