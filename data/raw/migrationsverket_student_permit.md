# Residence permit for studies in higher education

If you are planning to study at a university or university college in Sweden for longer than three months, and you are a citizen of a country outside the EU/EEA, you must apply for and receive a residence permit before traveling to Sweden.

## Requirements for a residence permit for higher education
To obtain a residence permit for higher education studies, you must:
1. Have been admitted to full-time accredited higher education studies in Sweden.
2. Have paid your tuition fee installment for the first semester, or have received a scholarship covering the tuition fee.
3. Have a valid passport that is valid for the entire intended permit duration.
4. Have comprehensive health insurance valid in Sweden if your permit is for less than one year. If admitted for at least one year, you will be registered in the Swedish Population Register upon arrival and covered by Swedish healthcare.
5. Be able to support yourself financially throughout the requested permit period.

## Maintenance Requirement (Financial Support)
For applications submitted in 2024 and 2025, the maintenance requirement for students is **SEK 10,314 per month**.
- For a 10-month academic year, you must show at least **SEK 103,140** in your personal bank account.
- The funds must be in your own personal bank account (not parents' or sponsor's account unless through an official government scholarship or bank loan in your name).
- Bank statements must not be older than one month from the application date.

## Working while studying
As an international student with a valid residence permit for higher education, you are permitted to work in Sweden alongside your studies without a separate work permit. However, your primary focus must be your full-time studies, and you must make acceptable academic progress (at least 15 higher education credits completed during your first year, and 22.5 credits in subsequent years) to qualify for a permit extension.

## Permit Duration and Extensions
A residence permit for studies is usually granted for one year at a time. If your study program is longer than one year, you must apply for an extension before your current permit expires (usually 30 to 60 days before expiration).
