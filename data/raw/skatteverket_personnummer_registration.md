# Moving to Sweden and registering in the Swedish Population Register (Folkbokföring)

If you move to Sweden with the intention of living here for **at least one year (12 months)**, you must generally register in the Swedish Population Register (*folkbokförd*). This is handled by the Swedish Tax Agency (*Skatteverket*).

## What is a Swedish Personal Identity Number (Personnummer)?
When you are registered in the population register, you are assigned a Swedish personal identity number (*personnummer*). The personnummer consists of your birth date followed by four digits (YYYYMMDD-XXXX). It is essential for opening bank accounts, getting BankID, receiving healthcare on subsidized terms, and signing contracts in Sweden.

## Requirements for Folkbokföring Registration
To register and receive a personnummer, you must:
1. **Physical Presence:** Visit a Skatteverket service office (*Statens servicecenter*) in person for an identity check with all family members moving with you.
2. **Intent to Reside for 12+ Months:** Prove that you have the right of residence or a residence permit valid for at least 12 months.
3. **Passports / ID:** Present original valid passports or national identity cards for all applicants.
4. **Civil Status Documents:** Present original marriage certificates and children's birth certificates if moving with family.
5. **Purpose Documentation:**
   - **Students:** Acceptance letter showing full-time program duration of $\ge 12$ months, proof of paid tuition or scholarship, and proof of comprehensive health insurance (if not covered automatically by 12+ month residence permit).
   - **Employees:** Employment contract (*anställningsavtal*) valid for at least one year.

## Processing Times
Notification of moving to Sweden (*Flytta till Sverige*) takes on average 2 to 6 weeks, though peak periods (August–October) may take longer. Skatteverket sends your decision and personnummer via postal mail to your registered Swedish address.
