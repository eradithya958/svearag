# Coordination numbers in Sweden (Samordningsnummer)

A coordination number (*samordningsnummer*) is an identification number for individuals who are not registered in the Swedish Population Register (*inte folkbokförda*) but need to interact with Swedish authorities or work in Sweden for less than one year.

## Structure of a Coordination Number
A coordination number is structured identically to a personnummer (10 or 12 digits), except that the number 60 is added to the day of birth. For example, if a person is born on the 15th of March 1995, the day is written as $15 + 60 = 75$, resulting in `19950375-XXXX`.

## Who Requests a Coordination Number?
You cannot apply for a coordination number on your own without an official authority request. It is requested on your behalf by:
- **Skatteverket:** If you are working in Sweden for less than six months or paying SINK tax.
- **Swedish Public Employment Service (Arbetsförmedlingen):** If looking for work.
- **Swedish Police (Polisen) or Swedish Transport Agency (Transportstyrelsen):** For driving licenses or legal proceedings.

## Levels of Identity Confirmation
Since September 2023, coordination numbers have three identity assurance levels:
1. **Confirmed identity (Styrkt identitet):** The person has visited in person and presented an original passport with biometric security.
2. **Probable identity (Görs sannolik):** Identity supported by secondary documents.
3. **Uncertain identity (Osäker identitet):** Identity could not be fully verified.

A coordination number expires after five years if it is not renewed.
